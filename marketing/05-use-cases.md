# Aoi - Use Cases & Scenarios

## Overview

Aoi works for all types of couples at different relationship stages. Here are specific scenarios showing how different couples use the app.

---

## Use Case 1: New Couples (0-6 months)

**Profile:**
- Dating 1-6 months
- Getting to know each other
- Building shared experiences
- Lots of "firsts"

**Pain Points:**
- Forgetting things they talked about wanting to do
- Losing track of date ideas
- No shared space for memories
- Everything scattered across texts and social apps

**How They Use Aoi:**

**Timeline:**
- First date milestone
- Screenshot of restaurants to try
- Photos from dates
- Inside jokes and funny moments
- "Getting to know you" notes

**Calendar:**
- Planning next dates
- Coordinating schedules
- Remembering each other's important events
- Tracking anniversaries (1 month, etc.)

**Example Moment:**
"Note: Alex hates cilantro but loves Thai food. Remember this for future dinner plans!"

**Success Story:**
"We used Aoi to keep track of all our firsts—first date, first trip, first time meeting parents. Now we have this beautiful timeline of our relationship growing. It's like our digital scrapbook." — Jamie & Jordan

**Key Benefits:**
- ✅ Never forget date ideas
- ✅ Track relationship milestones
- ✅ Build shared history from day one
- ✅ Archive if things don't work out (no awkwardness)

## Success Patterns Across All Use Cases

**What Successful Couples Do:**

1. **Weekly Ritual** — Review calendar and timeline together (15 min)
2. **Capture Immediately** — Add moments while they're fresh
3. **Use Search** — Find old screenshots when needed
4. **Export Regularly** — Backup memories monthly
5. **Archive Gracefully** — End relationships with dignity if needed

**Time Investment:**
- Power users: 10-15 min/day
- Casual users: 5 min, 2-3x/week
- Minimum viable: 5 min/week
