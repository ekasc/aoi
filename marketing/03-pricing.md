## FAQ - Pricing

### Q: Is it really a one-time payment?
**A:** Yes. Pay $9.99 once to create a relationship space. Your partner joins free. No monthly fees, no annual renewals. The relationship space works forever.

### Q: What if we break up?
**A:** Either partner can archive the relationship, making it read-only. You keep all memories. You both keep access to your own uploads. No additional fees.

### Q: Do I need to pay for each relationship?
**A:** Yes, each relationship space is $9.99. Most users only need one. If you start a new relationship later, you create a new space for $9.99.

### Q: Is the 5GB storage per person or shared?
**A:** Shared between both partners. For most couples, 5GB lasts 1-2 years of active use.

### Q: What happens when we run out of storage?
**A:** You can upgrade to a storage plan, or delete older media to free space. Text moments never count against storage.

### Q: Can I cancel the storage subscription?
**A:** Yes, anytime. You keep view access to all content. You can download everything. You just can't upload new media until you upgrade again or free space.

### Q: Do you offer discounts?
**A:** We don't run sales or discounts. The price is fair and consistent for everyone.

### Q: What about student discounts?
**A:** The $9.99 price is already accessible. We don't offer additional student discounts at this time.

### Q: Is there a family plan?
**A:** Aoi is designed for couples (two people). There's no family plan because the product is specifically for couples.

### Q: Can I get a refund if my partner doesn't join?
**A:** Yes. If your partner doesn't accept the invite within 30 days, you get a full refund automatically.

## Monetization Strategy Notes

### Why One-Time Purchase?

**Advantages:**
- ✅ Lower barrier to entry vs subscription
- ✅ No ongoing billing anxiety
- ✅ Aligns with "buy once, own forever" values
- ✅ Predictable revenue per relationship
- ✅ Simpler accounting

**Challenges:**
- ❌ No recurring revenue stream
- ❌ Must acquire new customers for growth
- ❌ Storage costs ongoing

**Mitigations:**
- Storage subscriptions for heavy users
- Word-of-mouth growth (couples tell couples)
- Optional premium features (post-MVP)

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Conservative Estimate:**
- Relationship creation: $9.99
- Storage upgrade (30% of users): $4.99/month × 12 = $59.88/year
- Average relationship duration: 2.5 years
- **LTV: $9.99 + ($59.88 × 0.3 × 2.5) = $54.90**

**Optimistic Estimate:**
- Relationship creation: $9.99
- Storage upgrade (50% of users): $4.99/month × 12 = $59.88/year
- Average relationship duration: 4 years
- **LTV: $9.99 + ($59.88 × 0.5 × 4) = $129.75**

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Targets

**Target CAC:** $5-15  
**Target LTV:CAC Ratio:** 3:1 or better

**Acquisition Channels:**
- App Store optimization (free)
- Content marketing (low cost)
- Word of mouth (free)
- Influencer partnerships ($500-2000)
- Paid ads ($2-5 per install)

## Legal & Financial

### Taxes

**Sales Tax:**
- Calculated based on location
- Shown at checkout
- Collected and remitted by Apple/Google (mobile)
- Self-remitted for web sales

**VAT (EU):**
- Included in displayed price
- MOSS scheme compliance

### Financial Sustainability

**Cost Structure:**
- Server costs: ~$0.10/GB/month
- Storage costs: ~$0.005/GB/month (R2)
- Payment processing: 30% (Apple/Google) or 2.9% + $0.30 (Stripe)
- Break-even: ~200 active relationships

**Unit Economics:**
- Revenue per relationship: $9.99 (avg)
- Cost per relationship (year 1): ~$2.50
- Gross margin: ~75%

- Terms of service

**We don't:**
- Hide fees
- Use dark patterns
