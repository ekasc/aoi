## Copy Style Guide

**Tone:** Warm, intimate, clear, trustworthy  
**Voice:** Conversational but professional  
**Person:** Second person ("you," "your")  
**Sentence Length:** Short, punchy sentences (15-20 words max)  
**Vocabulary:** Simple, accessible, no jargon  
**Emoji Usage:** Minimal, purposeful (💙 only)

**Words to Use:**
- ✅ Private, personal, intimate
- ✅ Moments, memories, story
- ✅ Simple, calm, quiet
- ✅ You, your, together
- ✅ Save, keep, preserve

**Words to Avoid:**
- ❌ Revolutionary, disruptive, innovative
- ❌ Synergy, holistic, seamless
- ❌ Users, consumers, demographic
- ❌ Leverage, utilize, optimize
- ❌ Buzzwords (AI, blockchain, etc.)

## Conversion Flow

**Visitor Journey:**
1. Lands on page (5-second test: Do they understand what Aoi is?)
2. Scans hero (Headline + CTA visible?)
3. Reads problem section (Do they relate?)
4. Views solution/features (Does it solve their problem?)
5. Checks pricing (Is it reasonable?)
6. Reads social proof (Do others like it?)
7. Reviews FAQ (Are their objections handled?)
8. **Conversion:** Clicks download

**Friction Points to Monitor:**
- High bounce on hero = unclear value proposition
- Drop-off at pricing = price too high or unclear
- No scroll = content not engaging
- FAQ most-viewed = objections not handled in main copy

## Success Metrics

**Primary KPIs:**
- Conversion rate (visitors → downloads): Target 5-10%
- Time on page: Target 2-3 minutes
- Scroll depth: Target 75%+
- CTA click-through: Target 10-15%

**Secondary KPIs:**
- Bounce rate: Target < 40%
- Return visitor rate
- Mobile vs desktop conversion
- Source attribution (organic, social, referral)

**Leading Indicators:**
- Hero CTA clicks
- Section scroll depth
- FAQ clicks
- Pricing card engagement
