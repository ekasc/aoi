# Aoi - How It Works

## The Simple 3-Step Process

### Step 1: Download & Create
**Time: 2 minutes**

1. Download Aoi from the App Store or Google Play
2. Create your account (email + verification code)
3. Choose a theme that feels right (Sunset Shore, Sea Glass, or Deep Ocean)

**What happens:**
- You get your own private account
- Theme applies to your device (your partner will see the same theme)
- You're ready to create your relationship space

**Visual:** Phone screen showing download → signup form → theme selector

---

### Step 2: Create Your Space & Invite
**Time: 1 minute**

1. Tap "Create Relationship Space"
2. Complete one-time $9.99 purchase
3. Generate an invite code
4. Share the code with your partner

**What happens:**
- Your relationship space is created
- You get a unique invite code (expires in 7 days)
- Share via text, WhatsApp, or any messaging app
- Your partner downloads Aoi and enters the code

**Visual:** Phone showing purchase flow → invite code screen → sharing options

---

### Step 3: Start Sharing Together
**Time: Ongoing**

1. Add your first moment (photo, note, or milestone)
2. Create a calendar event (date night, anniversary)
3. Explore together

**What happens:**
- Both partners can add moments anytime
- Calendar syncs in real-time
- Notifications when your partner adds something (optional)
- Build your shared history

**Visual:** Split screen showing both partners' phones adding moments

---

## Feature Walkthrough

### Using the Timeline

**Adding a Moment:**
1. Tap "+" button
2. Choose type (Photo, Note, Milestone, Date, Goal)
3. Add content
   - Photos: Select from gallery or take new
   - Notes: Write your memory
   - Milestones: Mark special dates
4. Add title (optional)
5. Save

**Viewing Your Timeline:**
- Scroll through chronological feed (newest first)
- Tap any moment to see details
- Search by keyword
- Filter by type

**Example Moments:**
- Screenshot of that restaurant you want to try
- Photo from your weekend trip
- Note: "Remember when we got lost and found that amazing coffee shop?"
- Milestone: 6 months together

**Visual:** Screen recording of adding a moment → scrolling timeline

---

### Using the Calendar

**Adding an Event:**
1. Tap "Calendar" tab
2. Tap "Add Event"
3. Enter details:
   - Title (e.g., "Dinner at Italian Place")
   - Date and time
   - Location (optional)
   - Label (Date, Work, Travel, etc.)
4. Save

**Viewing the Calendar:**
- Monthly grid shows both partners' events
- Tap a day to see agenda view
- Color-coded by who created it
- See event count badges on busy days

**Coordination Features:**
- See when your partner is free
- Plan around each other's schedules
- Set reminders for important dates
- Add recurring events (anniversaries)

**Example Events:**
- Date night every Friday
- Alex busy Wed 2-4pm (work meeting)
- Anniversary trip: March 15-20
- Weekly grocery run

**Visual:** Calendar grid view → adding event → both partners' schedules

---

### Managing Your Relationship

**Archiving (if needed):**
1. Go to Settings
2. Tap "Close this chapter"
3. Confirm archive
4. Relationship becomes read-only
5. Both partners keep access to memories

**Leaving:**
1. Go to Settings
2. Tap "Leave relationship"
3. Export your data (optional)
4. Confirm
5. You retain your uploads; partner keeps the space

**Starting Fresh:**
- Create a new relationship space anytime
- Previous relationships remain archived
- Each relationship is $9.99

**Visual:** Settings screen → archive confirmation → archived view

- 6 months

Once a month, export your data. Keep a backup. Memories are irreplaceable.

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't find that screenshot"
**Solution:** Use the search bar. Search by keyword, date, or type.

### "My partner hasn't joined yet"
**Solution:**
- Check that you sent the correct invite code
- Remind them it's free for them
- Code expires in 7 days—generate a new one if needed
- They need to download Aoi first

### "We're running out of storage"
**Solution:**
- Check storage usage in Settings
- Delete duplicate photos
- Upgrade to storage plan if needed
- Export and backup old content first

### "I accidentally archived the relationship"
**Solution:** Either partner can unarchive from Settings. Archived relationships remain read-only.

### "My moments aren't syncing"
**Solution:**
- Check internet connection
- Pull down to refresh
- Force close and reopen app
- Contact support if persistent
