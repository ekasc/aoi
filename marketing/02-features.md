# Aoi - Features & Capabilities

## Overview

Aoi provides couples with three core capabilities: capturing memories in a timeline, coordinating schedules through a shared calendar, and maintaining complete privacy with no public feeds or social features.

## Feature Categories

### 1. Timeline - Memory Capture

**Purpose:** Save and revisit important moments, big and small

**Capabilities:**
- **Moment Types:**
  - Photos (with optional location)
  - Short videos (10-20 seconds)
  - Text notes
  - Milestones (anniversaries, achievements)
  - Date entries
  - Goals (shared objectives)

- **Timeline Organization:**
  - Chronological feed (newest first)
  - Infinite scroll
  - Date-based navigation
  - Search by keyword
  - Filter by moment type

- **Rich Content:**
  - Add titles and descriptions
  - Tag locations (optional, rounded precision)
  - Attach multiple photos to one moment
  - Add reactions (limited set: heart, smile, etc.)

**User Value:**
- Never lose important screenshots or photos in chat threads
- Build a shared history together
- Revisit memories on anniversaries or special dates
- See your relationship grow over time

**Example Use Cases:**
- "Screenshot of that restaurant we want to try"
- "Photo from our first date"
- "Note about our inside joke"
- "Milestone: 6 months together"

---

### 2. Calendar - Planning & Coordination

**Purpose:** Coordinate schedules, plan dates, and track important dates

**Capabilities:**
- **Event Creation:**
  - Title and description
  - Start/end times
  - All-day events
  - Recurring events (anniversaries, birthdays)
  - Location (optional)
  - Labels/categories (Date, Work, Travel, etc.)

- **Shared View:**
  - Monthly grid view
  - Daily agenda view
  - See both partners' availability
  - Color-coded events (by creator)
  - Event count badges on calendar days

- **Event Management:**
  - Add events (either partner can add)
  - Edit events (creator can edit)
  - Delete events
  - Event notifications (optional)

**User Value:**
- Know when your partner is free
- Plan dates without back-and-forth texting
- Never forget important dates (anniversaries, birthdays)
- Coordinate schedules at a glance

**Example Use Cases:**
- "Dinner reservation Friday 7pm"
- "Alex busy Wed 2-4pm"
- "Anniversary trip planning"
- "Weekly date night reminder"

---

### 3. Privacy & Security

**Purpose:** Keep your relationship completely private

**Capabilities:**
- **No Public Sharing:**
  - No feeds
  - No likes or comments
  - No public profiles
  - No social features whatsoever

- **Access Control:**
  - Only two people per relationship space
  - Invite-only joining (via code)
  - No discoverability by other users
  - End-to-end encryption for media (in transit and at rest)

- **Data Ownership:**
  - You own everything you upload
  - Export all data anytime
  - Delete your account (removes your data)
  - Archive relationship (keeps data read-only)

- **No Tracking:**
  - No third-party analytics
  - No ad tracking
  - No behavioral profiling
  - Minimal logging (for debugging only)

**User Value:**
- True privacy without worry
- No pressure to perform for others
- No algorithmic manipulation
- Your data stays yours

**Privacy Promise:**
"We don't sell your data. We don't show ads. We don't track you. Your relationship is yours alone."

---

### 4. Media Management

**Purpose:** Store and organize photos and videos securely

**Capabilities:**
- **Upload:**
  - Direct-to-cloud upload (fast)
  - Automatic compression (optional)
  - Background upload
  - Progress indicators
  - Retry on failure

- **Storage:**
  - 5GB included per relationship
  - Optional storage upgrades
  - Cloudflare R2 (reliable, fast)
  - Automatic backup

- **Organization:**
  - Attached to timeline moments
  - Browse by date
  - Grid view of all media
  - Full-resolution download

- **Privacy Controls:**
  - Strip EXIF data (optional)
  - Location rounding (~1km precision)
  - No public URLs
  - Signed URLs only (short expiration)

**User Value:**
- Fast uploads even on slow connections
- Media organized with context (not just a photo dump)
- Never run out of phone storage
- Keep photos safe and private

---

### 5. Relationship Management

**Purpose:** Handle relationship lifecycle gracefully

**Capabilities:**
- **Creation:**
  - One-time $9.99 purchase
  - Generate invite code
  - Partner joins free

- **Active State:**
  - Both partners can add/edit content
  - Real-time sync
  - Activity indicators (optional)

- **Archive:**
  - Either partner can archive
  - Becomes read-only
  - Memories preserved
  - Can be unarchived (by mutual agreement)

- **Leave:**
  - Either partner can leave
  - Retains ownership of uploaded content
  - Other partner keeps relationship space
  - Data export available

- **Delete:**
  - Account deletion removes your data
  - Partner's data remains
  - 30-day grace period for export

**User Value:**
- Relationships end—handle it with dignity
- Keep memories even after breakups
- No awkward "who gets the Dropbox" situations
- Clear ownership and control

## Technical Specifications

**Platform Support:**
- iOS 15+
- Android 10+
- Web (progressive web app)

**Performance Targets:**
- App launch < 2 seconds
- Timeline scroll 60fps
- Photo upload < 5 seconds (on 4G)
- Calendar load < 1 second

**Storage:**
- 5GB base (approximately 2,500 photos)
- Expansion: 25GB, 100GB tiers
- Compression: Automatic, optional

**Sync:**
- Real-time (WebSocket)
- Offline support (SQLite)
- Conflict resolution (last-write-wins)
- Background sync
