# Aoi - Our Story

## Why We Built Aoi

Every couple has moments they want to remember.

The screenshot of that restaurant you both want to try. The photo from your first date. The note about your partner's coffee order. The anniversary plans. The inside jokes.

But in a world of endless feeds, group chats, and algorithmic noise, these moments get lost. Buried under memes and work messages. Forgotten in the digital void.

**We built Aoi to give couples a quieter way to share their story.**

---

## The Problem We Saw

We looked at existing "couple apps" and found:
- ❌ Subscription fatigue ($3/month forever)
- ❌ Cluttered with stickers, games, and features nobody uses
- ❌ Social pressure (performance metrics, feeds)
- ❌ Not truly private (ads, data mining)
- ❌ No graceful way to end things when relationships do

We wanted something different.

Something calm.
Something intentional.
Something that respects the intimacy of a relationship.

---

## What Makes Aoi Different

**One simple price.** Pay once ($9.99), own forever. No subscriptions. No ads. No tricks.

**Truly private.** No feeds. No likes. No algorithms. Just you and your partner in a space that's yours alone.

**Intentionally minimal.** One timeline. One calendar. No bloat. No confusion.

**Designed for reality.** Relationships end. Aoi lets you archive with grace—keeping memories without daily reminders.

**No AI.** Human moments captured by humans. No creepy analysis. No generated content.

---

## The Name

**Aoi (青い)** means "blue" in Japanese.

Blue like clear water. Blue like calm skies. Blue like trust.

In many cultures, blue represents:
- Clarity and calm
- Trust and loyalty
- Depth and stability
- The space between sea and sky

**The space between.**

That's what Aoi is. The quiet space where your relationship lives. Between the chaos of daily life and the noise of the internet.

## Frequently Asked Questions (About Us)

**Q: Are you a big company?**
A: No. We're intentionally small—a tiny team who cares deeply about building something meaningful.

**Q: How do you make money?**
A: One-time purchases ($9.99 per relationship). Optional storage subscriptions for heavy users. No ads. No data selling.

**Q: Will you sell the company?**
A: We have no plans to sell. If that ever changed, we'd ensure user data protection in any acquisition.

**Q: What if you shut down?**
A: You'd have 90 days to export your data. We promise not to disappear overnight.

**Q: Are you hiring?**
A: Sometimes. Check careers@aoi.app for openings.

**Q: Can I invest?**
A: We're bootstrapped and not seeking investment. We prefer independence over growth-at-all-costs.

**Q: Why no AI features?**
A: We believe relationships are human. AI-generated content feels fake. We want authentic moments between real people.

**Q: What's next for Aoi?**
A: We're focused on perfecting the core experience. Maybe print-on-demand photo books in the future. Maybe not. We're being intentional.
