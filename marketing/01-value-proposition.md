# Aoi - Value Proposition & Core Messaging

## Product Identity

**Name:** Aoi  
**Tagline:** "Keep the moments that matter"  
**Positioning:** A private, shared space for couples to save memories, plan together, and stay connected without the noise of social media.

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Gen Z / Millennial couples (ages 22-35)  
**Secondary:** Long-distance couples, newlyweds, couples in new relationships  
**Psychographics:** 
- Value privacy and intentionality
- Dislike social media performance pressure
- Want a calm, personal archive
- Add content occasionally, revisit often
- Prioritize quality time over constant connection

## Core Value Proposition

**The Problem We Solve:**
Couples lose track of important moments, date ideas, and plans in endless chat threads, scattered notes apps, and social media feeds. The things that matter get buried.

**Our Solution:**
A dedicated, private space where you and your partner can save screenshots, plan dates, track memories, and coordinate schedules—without algorithms, feeds, or public sharing.

**Key Differentiators:**
1. **Truly Private** - No feeds, no likes, no public sharing. Just you two.
2. **Relationship as a Chapter** - Archive or end relationships gracefully without losing memories
3. **Calm by Design** - Intentionally minimal, distraction-free interface
4. **Media Ownership** - You own what you upload, always
5. **No AI** - Human moments captured by humans

## Tone & Voice Guidelines

**Warm but Clear:** Use conversational language that feels intimate but remains easy to understand
**Active Voice:** "Save your moments" not "Your moments are saved"
**Benefit-Focused:** Lead with what users get, not how it works
**Inclusive:** "You two" instead of gendered language
**Honest:** No buzzwords, no overpromising, no marketing fluff

## Keywords for SEO

**Secondary:** anniversary tracker, date planner, relationship timeline, couples journal, love diary  
**Long-tail:** app for couples to share photos, private space for couples, relationship memory book

## Visual Direction

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Cyan (#4ECDC4) - represents clarity and calm
- Secondary: Warm coral/sunset tones
- Background: Warm cream/beige tones
- Overall: Beach-inspired, sunset-to-dusk warmth

**Imagery Style:**
- Authentic couples in candid moments (not posed stock photos)
- Soft, warm lighting (golden hour aesthetic)
- Intimate but not explicit
- Diverse representation
- Show phones/devices minimally, focus on emotions

**UI Mockups:**
- Show real app screens with sample content
- Use glass morphism cards
- Display both light and dark mode themes

## Competitive Positioning

**Vs. Between (existing app):**
- Aoi is calmer, more minimalist
- Aoi treats relationships as chapters (can be archived)
- Aoi has no chat/messaging (keeps it focused on memories)

**Vs. Shared Photo Albums (Google Photos, iCloud):**
- Aoi is purpose-built for couples
- Aoi includes calendar/scheduling
- Aoi is completely private (no sharing accidentally)

**Vs. Social Media (Instagram, Facebook):**
- Aoi has no public sharing
- Aoi has no likes/comments/performance metrics
- Aoi is about the relationship, not public perception

## Conversion Optimization Notes

**Above-the-fold priorities:**
1. Clear headline stating value
2. Visual showing the outcome
3. Primary CTA button
4. Trust indicator (free download, privacy promise)

**Mobile considerations:**
- CTA button must be thumb-sized (min 44px)
- Headline must be readable at small sizes
- Remove secondary CTAs on mobile to reduce friction
- Simplify social proof to one line

**Page load priorities:**
- Hero image under 200KB
- Fonts loaded with display=swap
- Critical CSS inline
- Lazy load below-fold images

## Brand Story (For About Page)

Aoi was created for couples who want a quieter way to share their story.

In a world of endless feeds and algorithmic noise, we wanted to build something calmer. A private space where screenshots don't get buried, date ideas don't get forgotten, and memories don't disappear into chat history.

Every relationship deserves a place that's just for the two of you. No performance. No pressure. Just your story, saved.

The name "Aoi" means blue in Japanese—the color of clear water, calm skies, and trust.
