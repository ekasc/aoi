How to read this sheet. Three destinations — Story / Together / Plans — with Space and settings behind the avatar. Photo, Note, and Voice compose from the floating action; letter compose sits contextually under Letters and plan compose under Plans. No generic six-choice sheet, no kept-count metrics or streaks, and no required theme, anniversary date, partner name, or space name.

Onboarding runs welcome → sign-in → one short setup screen → Story with an inline first-memory invitation; after the first save, the app offers the native invitation share. Joining preserves the invite through sign-in into a privacy-safe confirmation, then lands in Story on real partner content. Invalid, full, or expired invites offer recovery. Theme and date details live later in Space. Authentication is never bypassed, and invite metadata is never shown before sign-in.

Palette studies keep the same layout for colour comparison only and are not alternative designs. Source critique and concept remain clearly identified: earlier screenshot attempts have not established the app’s actual visual appearance.
